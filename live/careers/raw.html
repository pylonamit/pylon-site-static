Mortgages have failed America.

The mortgage market is $13T but the infrastructure underneath it hasn’t changed in decades. The way loans are manufactured, priced, and delivered is inefficient and expensive. And that cost gets passed to borrowers every single time.

Billions have been poured into vertical SaaS, point solutions, and digital front ends. None of it touched the real problem. It actually made origination more expensive.

When we decided to rebuild mortgage infrastructure from the ground up and to turn mortgage into an API call, people thought we were out of our minds. Some still do. We’ve learned to run on that kind of doubt.

Pylon is not a typical fintech company. We don’t come from the mortgage industry. We came in from the outside, got obsessed with the problem, and built something the industry said couldn’t be built. We’re opinionated, ship fast, and say what we think. We don’t have patience for the way things have always been done.

If you’re the kind of person who does their best work when the problem is hard and the stakes are real, this is your place.

Join us.
